The Best Ducking TD Game of All Time

A chaotic tower defense game originally created for a game jam. Place your defenses, manage your resources, and survive increasingly wild waves of enemies while leaning into over-the-top duck-themed chaos. Developed in 48 hours, currently in beta, with a big update and rerelease planned.

Trick or Treat

A Halloween micro-game where you roam a spooky neighborhood, knock on doors, and choose whether to be a wholesome trick-or-treater or cause trouble. Your choices affect karma, which changes your odds of treats, tricks, and stranger outcomes as the night goes on.

❮ ❯

SpellFister

Doom-style magic first-person shooter. Fight waves of enemies, level up, and gain upgrades. Currently in beta with a huge update and rerelease planned for late 2026 as a full-blown roguelite.

❮ ❯

Recycle Me!

In this physics-based puzzle game, play as a little character on a rooftop in a city where waste falls from the sky. Your goal is simple: toss the garbage into their respective bins without falling off or allowing too much garbage to pile up! Planned for release September 2024.
